About Us

Who we are, what we craft for Singapore trails, and why we started.

Our Story

Founded in Singapore in November 2026, No Limits Sports began with a simple goal: creating outdoor footwear and gear built for tropical humidity and sudden rain—light enough for daily city runs and tough enough for wet granite trails.

From weekend trials across Bukit Timah and MacRitchie to our design bench at No Limits Sports Studio, we craft breathable hiking and jogging shoes alongside durable trail packs, hydration bottles, and caps made for everyday movement.

Mission

To equip Singapore hikers and daily runners with breathable, wet-grip footwear that transitions effortlessly from muddy ridge climbs to city paths.

Vision

A local outdoor community where every runner and weekend trekker can step onto any trail with reliable comfort, honest sizing, and zero excess bulk.

Values

Real-route testing before release, quick-dry tropical durability, and straightforward in-person fitting advice at our Singapore studio.
